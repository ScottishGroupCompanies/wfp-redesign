# About Window Film Philadelphia

Window Film Philadelphia is the Delaware Valley office of Scottish Window Tinting, a national window film company founded by Martin Faith in 2008.

## Our Story

Martin Faith moved from Glasgow, Scotland — home of the Glasgow School of Art — to Colorado in 1991, bringing decades of traditional stained glass craft. He founded Scottish Stained Glass in 1993, which grew into one of the largest custom stained glass studios in the United States.

In 2008, Martin installed energy-efficient window film on his own home and watched his utility bills drop dramatically. His average daily utility cost fell from $8.98 to $2.54 after applying solar control film to existing glass. That firsthand proof — real savings on a real house — convinced him that window film was a viable business. He founded Scottish Window Tinting that same year.
