# Window Film Philadelphia

Window Film Philadelphia provides professional window film installation for homes and businesses across Philadelphia and the Delaware Valley. We are the Philadelphia office of Scottish Window Tinting, locally owned and operating since 2008.

## Services

- **Residential Window Film**: Home tinting for UV protection, privacy, safety, decorative film, and energy savings. Serving rowhomes, condos, and single-family homes across Philadelphia.
- **Commercial Window Tinting**: Solar control, anti-graffiti, safety and security, decorative and branding film for offices, retail, restaurants, and commercial buildings.
- **Government & Public Buildings**: Security window film for schools, government facilities, mass transit, and public spaces.

## Benefits

- **Energy Savings**: Reduce cooling costs by blocking up to 80% of solar heat gain through existing glass.
- **UV Protection**: Block 99% of harmful UV rays that cause fading to furniture, floors, and artwork.
- **Privacy Film**: One-way daytime privacy without sacrificing natural light.
- **Safety & Security Film**: Holds shattered glass together to slow forced entry and reduce flying-glass injuries.
- **Glare Reduction**: Cut screen glare and improve comfort in offices and homes.
- **Decorative Film**: Frosted, etched, and custom-branded glass for privacy and design.

## Applications

We install window film in: homes and condos, office buildings, hotels, restaurants and bars, retail storefronts, healthcare facilities, government buildings, mass transit, schools and universities, museums and libraries, churches, and secured buildings.

## Products
