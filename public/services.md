# Window Film Services in Philadelphia

Window Film Philadelphia offers professional window film installation for residential, commercial, and government properties across the Delaware Valley.

## Residential Window Film

Window film for Philadelphia homes and condos. Reduce heat, block UV rays, add privacy, and improve comfort without replacing your windows.

- **UV Protection Film**: Block 99% of harmful UV rays. Protects furniture, floors, and artwork from fading.
- **Energy Savings Film**: Reduce cooling costs by blocking up to 80% of solar heat gain through existing glass.
- **Privacy Film**: One-way daytime privacy without sacrificing natural light. Ideal for rowhomes and street-facing windows.
- **Safety & Security Film**: Holds shattered glass together to slow forced entry and reduce injury from flying glass.
- **Decorative Film**: Frosted, etched, and custom-branded glass for privacy and design.

## Commercial Window Film

Window film for Philadelphia offices, retail spaces, restaurants, and commercial buildings.

- **Solar Control Film**: Cut heat and glare to improve occupant comfort and reduce HVAC costs.
- **Anti-Graffiti Film**: Protect glass from vandalism and scratches. Removable sacrificial layer.
- **Safety & Security Film**: Holds glass together on impact. Meets safety glazing standards.
- **Decorative & Branding Film**: Frosted, etched, and custom-branded film for privacy and corporate identity.
- **Casper Cloaking Film**: Blocks screen visibility from outside conference rooms while remaining transparent to those inside.

## Government & Public Buildings

Security and safety window film for schools, government facilities, mass transit, museums, and public spaces.

- **School Security Film**: Shatter-resistant window film designed to delay forced entry and protect occupants.
- **Blast Mitigation Film**: Holds glass together during explosive events, reducing flying-glass injuries.
- **Bird Strike Prevention Film**: Visible patterns that help birds see glass and avoid collisions.
