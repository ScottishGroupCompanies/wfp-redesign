## 1. 3M Sun Control Window Film — Prestige Series

**Technology:** Non-metallized, multi-layer optical film (~200 nano-layers). No metal = no signal interference, no corrosion, near-neutral aesthetics.

**Key specs (single-pane 1/4" clear glass):**
- IR Rejection: up to 97% (at 900–1,000 nm band) ✅ 3M.com
- UV Block: 99.9% ✅ 3M.com
- VLT range: PR20 (21%) → PR70 (69%) ✅ 3M.com product listing
- Exterior visible reflectance: ~5–9% (near-neutral, low mirror effect) ✅
- TSER: varies by SKU; PR70 ~66%, lower SKUs ~55–60%
- Carbon negative within ~6 months of installation ✅ 3M GHG Protocol analysis

**Best for:** Commercial and residential where max visible light + neutral aesthetics + top heat/IR rejection is needed. South/west-facing offices, luxury residential, curtain-wall facades.

**Warranty:** Residential: Limited Lifetime | Commercial: 15 years ✅ 3M.com

## 3. 3M Thinsulate™ Climate Control Window Film (CC75)

**Technology:** Dual-season low-emissivity film. Reduces heat gain in summer AND retains interior heat in winter by improving the window's U-value. Launched 2016.

**Key specs:**
- VLT: 74% ✅ (near-neutral, high clarity)
- Film thickness: 3.5 mils
- U-value improvement on single-pane: 1.03 → 0.62 (40% improvement) ✅ multimedia.3m.com TDS
- SHGC improvement on single-pane: 0.82 → 0.53 (35% improvement) ✅
- U-value improvement on double-pane: 0.47 → 0.35 (26% improvement) ✅
- Exterior VLR: 15% | Interior VLR: ~12%
- Cost comparison: performs like adding a second pane at ~1/10 the cost of window replacement ✅ 3M marketing claims (installer-cited, not independently verified)
- TSER: Not a primary spec for Thinsulate — U-value is the selling metric

**Best for:** Mixed-climate markets (Zone 4A like Philadelphia) where winter heat retention AND summer heat rejection both matter. Old single-pane rowhomes, historic buildings, any property with original windows.

**Warranty:** Residential: Limited Lifetime | Commercial: 15 years ✅ epdwindowfilm.com (authorized dealer)

## 4. 3M Scotchshield™ Safety & Security Window Film — Ultra Series

**Technology:** Multi-layered micro-laminated tear-resistant polyester. Optically clear. Holds shattered glass together on impact. Available standalone (clear) or combined with solar control films (Ultra Night Vision, Ultra Prestige combos).

**Key specs — Ultra S800 (flagship clear safety film):**
- Thickness: 8 mils ✅ (product name = mil thickness: S200=2mil, S400=4mil, S600=6mil, S800=8mil)
- VLT: 87% (near-invisible) ✅ 3M.com
- UV Block: 99.9% ✅ 3M.com
- TSER: 21% (minimal solar control — this is a safety film, not a solar film) ✅
- Exterior VLR: 9% | Interior VLR: 9%
- IPA Sealant: Required when used for blast, windstorm, or break-and-entry mitigation ✅ 3M official disclaimer
- Applications: spontaneous glass breakage, break-and-entry, seismic, explosions (commercial/govt only), windstorm

**Available series:**
- S200 (2 mil) — basic safety, interior only
- S400 (4 mil) — enhanced tear resistance
- S600 (6 mil) — commercial standard
- S800 (8 mil) — maximum protection, government/high-security
- Ultra Night Vision S25 — S-film + NV25 solar control in one
- Ultra Prestige S70 — S-film + PR70 solar control in one

**Best for:** Retail storefronts (smash-and-grab), schools, government buildings, healthcare, historic buildings with irreplaceable glass, any property in high-crime or high-weather-risk areas.

**Warranty:** Residential: Limited Lifetime | Commercial: 12 years ✅ 3M.com
⚠️ Note: "THESE FILMS DO NOT PREVENT PROPERTY DAMAGE, PERSONAL INJURY, OR DEATH" — must include this caveat if citing protective properties.

## 5. 3M Fasara™ Decorative Glass Finishes

**Technology:** Decorative architectural film applied to glass surfaces. Creates frosted, etched, geometric, patterned, and opaque glass effects without replacing glass. Applied wet (repositionable during install, permanent once cured).

**Key specs:**
- 100+ patterns available ✅ 3M.com
- VLT: varies by pattern — translucent options ~40–70% VLT; opaque patterns 0% ✅
- No significant solar control performance — this is a decorative/privacy product
- Can be combined with custom digital printing for branded glass
- Removable/replaceable (vs. permanent glass treatments)
- Available: frosted, etch, linen, bamboo, geometric, privacy gradient, mirror, metallic, wood grain patterns

**Best for:** Office partitions, conference rooms, lobbies, retail storefronts, healthcare privacy glass, branded environments, anywhere a frosted or etched glass aesthetic is desired without the cost of replacement glass.

**Warranty:** 3 years (residential and commercial) ✅ — shorter than solar films due to aesthetic/decorative nature

## 6. 3M Daylight Redirecting Film (DRF)

**Technology:** Microstructured prismatic surface film applied to upper window zone (typically top 1–2 feet of window). Redirects incoming sunlight upward toward ceiling and deep into interior — extending natural daylight penetration up to 40 feet from the window. ✅ 3M.com

**Key specs:**
- Daylight penetration: up to 40 feet from window ✅ 3M.com
- Best orientation: south-facing windows at latitudes above approximately 35°N ✅
- VLT of film itself: ~46% (redirecting zone) — the film's prismatic structure means standard VLT measurement is not the primary performance metric
- Does NOT significantly reduce glare or heat in the lower window zone — pair with solar control film if heat/glare are also concerns
- Reduces electric lighting energy use in daylight zone
- LEED EQ credit eligible for daylighting ✅

**Best for:** Open-plan offices, classrooms, healthcare facilities, any deep-floor-plate commercial building where natural light doesn't reach interior workspaces. Reduces artificial lighting load and improves occupant wellbeing metrics.

**Warranty:** 10 years commercial ✅ (shorter than solar films — prismatic structure is more complex)
⚠️ Warranty source: 3M dealer documentation — verify before publishing.
