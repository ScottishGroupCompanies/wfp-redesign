- Body: Solar control films block up to 99% of UV radiation (UVA + UVB). UV exposure causes fading of hardwood floors, upholstery, artwork, and retail merchandise. Philadelphia retail corridors on Walnut Street and Chestnut Street see direct sun exposure across large storefront windows. High UV-blocking solar films protect inventory and reduce fading maintenance costs without requiring tinted glass.
- Internal link: "retail storefronts" → /applications/retail/

- Image: `sol-window-film-philadelphia-accb-glare.jpg`

- Body: Screen glare costs productivity — a well-cited issue in open-plan offices where employees resort to blinds and artificial lighting, eliminating the benefits of a windowed environment entirely. This means Philadelphia offices in University City and Center City can keep their city views while eliminating monitor glare. According to the International Window Film Association (IWFA), spectrally selective films consistently rank as the most requested commercial window film product category nationwide.

Solar control window films can contribute to LEED credits under Energy & Atmosphere (EA) and Indoor Environmental Quality (EQ) categories.
