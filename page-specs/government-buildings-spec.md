## Unique Angle
Philadelphia's civic building stock spans 1890s–1960s masonry with aging single-pane windows — a combination of preservation constraints and modern security/energy mandates. GSA compliance, LEED goals, post-9/11 blast standards (UFC 4-010-01), and Pennsylvania Act 44 security requirements drive demand. Unlike private commercial buildings, government procurement requires documented standards compliance.

## Benefit Rows (4)
1. **Blast Mitigation & Safety** — GSA UFC 4-010-01 blast-rated film, anti-fragmentation, forced entry delay at government entries

## Case Study
Location: Center City Philadelphia — Municipal government office building
Challenge: West-facing offices with intense afternoon heat gain; workstation screen glare; privacy concerns for permit-processing offices adjacent to public corridor
Solution: 3M Prestige 70 solar control on west-facing glass; LLumar reflective privacy on corridor-facing office glass
Result: Measurable comfort improvement; glare eliminated on staff screens; privacy maintained in permit areas — zero disruption to public-facing office hours
