## Brand Context

- **Full name:** Hüper Optik™ (use "Huper Optik" without umlaut in page copy for SEO)
- **Owner:** Eastman Chemical Company (same parent as LLumar and Vista)
- **Flagship tech:** Patented dual-layer Titanium Nitride (TiN) ceramic coating — US patent awarded
- **Key differentiator:** 100% metal-free ceramic — no signal interference (Wi-Fi, GPS, cellular, keyfobs)
- **Signal safety quote:** Use this in Why section: "The ceramic composition ensures no interference with wireless signals, GPS, or electronic key fobs," according to Huper Optik.
- **Heritage:** Named after Joseph von Fraunhofer (1787–1826), German optics pioneer
